Executive Summary

The 90-Day Digital Asset Communications Sprint: Interactive Playbook is a turnkey system which provides plug-and-play resources for your firm and advisors, for high-touch communication with clients. Its purpose is to instill confidence in these digital asset investments, reclaiming advisor capacity through ready-to-use educational mini-courses and original Weekly News Perspectives.

This interactive playbook contains everything needed to deploy a professional, compliance-conscious educational campaign over 90 days. The kit includes 3 email mini-courses (ETH and BTC email course for clients, 3 Mental Models (ETH & BTC discussions for advisors).

It also includes a slide deck, speaker notes, and script for office or firm-wide presentations, plus upon purchase a Loom video walkthrough. The Interactive Playbook will house the full text of these materials for forwarding within the firm or to clients.

The Weekly News Perspectives is a 10-week series that begins after clients have received the courses.

The Email Template Kit includes sample emails to introduce the courses, Weekly News Perspectives, respond to urgent news or market events, and to onboard new clients to digital asset education.

The Branding Kit is for use with your firm emails, by advisors or the marketing/ops team.

The Compliance Review Package will house all materials to be reviewed by Compliance in one pdf. The Evergreen Asset Library keeps all issues of the Weekly News Perspective in one place to be used as needed.

Increase operational capacity, reduce time burden on advisors, building trust with clients in a scalable and evergreen system that will be deployed according to firm or advisor discretion.

90-Day Implementation Guide

The rollout is designed to be deployed over 90 days, structured in three 30-day phases to maximize impact while maintaining sustainable momentum.

Pre-launch

Firm does compliance review and approves 5-week content calendar for Weekly News Perspectives.

Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1–6)

Weeks 1–2: Launch Ethereum Crash course, then Bitcoin Navigator email course, one per week.

Weeks 3–6: Establish Weekly News Perspectives cadence. Advisors may send 3 Mental Models emails to clients who panic about market volatility.

Week 6: Approve content calendar for second 5-week News Perspectives series.

Phase 2: Expansion (Weeks 7–10)

Weeks 7–8: Continue Weekly News Perspectives cadence with approved content from second series.

Weeks 9–10: Firm conducts feedback review if desired; Firm may analyze performance data, optimize email sequences, and refine Weekly News Perspectives topics based on client and advisor feedback. Scale successful topics.

Phase 3: Optimization (Weeks 11–12)

Weeks 11–12: Establish evergreen content library; optional planning call in week 11 to continue Weekly News Perspectives.

The Panic Cycle

The Problem

Each swing adds to cumulative anxiety

Clients don't just react to today's move. They carry forward every previous scare. After investors add a volatile asset to their portfolio, even small dips trigger panic emails—because the emotional bill has compounded.

Ongoing

The Communication Flywheel

Problem: When digital assets whipsaw, clients panic. Advisors get pulled into the same three loops, "Why is this falling?" "Are circumstances different?" "Should we sell?"

Mechanism: Create a shared lens, then reinforce it. Advisors use the courses to shift from price talk to function as clients move through the Sprint. Weekly News Perspectives turns down the noise.

Promise: Questions don't disappear but the tone changes.

Motion: Advisors reframe volatility, client calls shift to confident, curious check-ins. Advisors use experience and feedback for new digital asset client onboarding.

Addressing Concerns

Common Objections

Will clients want the email courses? Will they read the Weekly News Perspectives?

Experience with the email courses and similar offers is that site visitors tend to opt in at a rate up to 70% for this type of offer vs 1–3% for a request for their email without the offer. Many TradFi holders of crypto ETFs know little about ETH or Bitcoin apart from the symbol.

My clients don't read long emails, so will they read the email courses?

The email courses are intended to be light reading, give anecdotes, tell a story. They can also be skimmed, with clear subheads that convey what's contained. The emails are meant to entertain as well as inform.

Will the idea that clients should know more about ETH & BTC create more anxiety?

The object of the email courses is to calm investor anxiety in a narrative way, by using examples of how new technologies and innovations were not well understood, and how they become a part of our accepted experience almost invisibly. So no, the message is that knowing more makes you less reactive to short-term events.

How will clients view the Weekly News Perspectives?

The Weekly News Perspectives are written as a continuation of the style of the email courses, so clients anticipate continuing information, context, and updates. Knowing more is a positive for the investor: to make better decisions and to feel empowered to decide allocations, not just stay calm in turbulent weeks.

Published

What happened: Bitcoin reached a new all-time high this week, crossing $85,000 for the first time. Headlines ranged from "Bitcoin to the moon!" to "Bubble warning signs."

  • Volatility is actually lower than in previous bull cycles

Your takeaway: If you're holding Bitcoin as part of a diversified strategy, this is validation—not a reason to panic or trade. Stay focused on your long-term plan.
